Our story

Nine years of private nights

Barcelona Boys Agency specialises in professional male entertainment for private events across Barcelona and Catalunya.

Founded in Barcelona, we represent a small, carefully selected team of performers: dancers, models and entertainers who bring polish, charisma and professionalism to every booking. Each performer is interviewed, auditioned and trained by our team before joining the roster.

Every booking is handled personally by our events coordinators, from first enquiry to final performance.
